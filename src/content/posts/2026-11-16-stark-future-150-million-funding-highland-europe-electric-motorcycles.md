Stark Future, the Barcelona-based maker of VARG electric motorcycles, has closed a financing round worth more than €150 million in total, [The EV Report says](https://theevreport.com/stark-future-financing-round-highland-europe), citing the company's announcement on 16 November. The deal values the company at €1.2 billion before the new money.

## Who invested

Highland Europe led the round, joined by Morgan Stanley Investment Management's 1GT climate private equity strategy, the Spanish investment firm Axon Partners Group, existing shareholders and friends and family. Stark says the round was oversubscribed. A second closing remains open for more strategic partners, including Lombard Odier, and Stifel advised the company.

## What the money is for

Stark says it will use the funds for global expansion and new electric platforms, moving into motorcycle categories it describes as a €90 billion market, compared with about €3 billion for the global off-road segment it sells into now.

## Company claims

Founded in 2020, Stark designs and builds the VARG range of motocross, enduro and supermoto bikes at a site of about 20,000 square metres outside Barcelona. The company says the United States is now its largest market by volume and that it is profitable. It also claims the VARG is the world's best-selling enduro motorcycle and that it has taken about 5% of the global off-road market in three years. These are the company's own figures; they were not independently verified in the announcement.
