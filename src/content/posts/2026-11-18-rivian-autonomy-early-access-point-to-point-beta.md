Rivian has begun inviting owners into its Autonomy Early Access Program, a beta for the point-to-point assisted driving software it is developing, [InsideEVs reports](https://autos.yahoo.com/ev-and-future-tech/articles/rivian-inviting-owners-beta-test-220305694.html). The system is "eyes-on", meaning the driver still has to watch the road.

## Who can join

Rivian is not opening the beta to everyone who pays. According to the report, the requirements Rivian has published include:

- living in California or Florida for now, with owners elsewhere able to register interest for later expansion
- owning an R2 or an R1 on the Gen 2 platform; Gen 1 R1 owners are not eligible
- a paid Autonomy+ subscription, with Rivian also looking at how often the owner already uses its driver-assistance features
- a good driving safety score, based on in-car events such as forward collision warnings

InsideEVs, citing RivianTrackr, says the programme terms also allow Rivian to look at an applicant's driving history for offences such as DUI, reckless driving or at-fault crashes. Participants are expected to use the point-to-point features weekly, answer surveys, log issues and share certain data, including location and camera data, under a non-disclosure agreement.

## What Rivian has said

A Rivian spokesperson confirmed the programme to InsideEVs. Rivian has not set a limit on how many owners it will enrol or how long the beta will run, but says it plans to scale it quickly and later offer the features on new-vehicle test drives. Eligible owners register through their Rivian account.

## What comes next

Rivian's AI and Autonomy Day on 9 December is expected to be "all about" Autonomy+, according to the report, so the fuller feature list and any wider rollout plan may come then.
