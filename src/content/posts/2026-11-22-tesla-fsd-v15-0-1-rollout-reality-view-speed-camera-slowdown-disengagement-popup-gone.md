The FSD update Tesla owners have been waiting for is now arriving in garages. Tesla investor and commentator Sawyer Merritt [posted the release notes on X](https://x.com/SawyerMerritt/status/2124235497442558376) on Saturday, and hours later [shared that v15 was downloading](https://x.com/SawyerMerritt/status/2124271378073530460) on his own Model Y.

## Who gets it first

According to [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/software-updates/version/2026.38.6.11/release-notes), FSD (Supervised) v15.0.1 arrives in software update 2026.38.6.11. The first wave is going to HW4 cars, starting with the new Model Y. Tesla usually widens a rollout over days or weeks, so not every eligible car will see it straight away.

## What is new

The headline claim in Tesla's notes is an "upgraded vision encoder shared between FSD, Robotaxi, and Optimus," trained on millions of internet videos. Tesla says this improves the critical intervention rate by 25x. That is Tesla's own figure and has not been independently tested.

Other changes in the notes include:

- **Tesla Reality View**, a new real-time visualisation from a virtual camera above the car that shows what FSD plans to do, using less computing power than before.
- **Speed Camera Slowdown**. FSD can now slow down when it detects a speed camera. If you cancel the slowdown twice at the same camera, the car saves that location and stops slowing there.
- **Bike Rack Mode**, which lets you use FSD and Autosteer with a bike rack or hitch fitted. The car pulls over near your destination and does not reverse.
- Better detection of road signs, small animals, debris, potholes and deep standing water, and improved driver monitoring with hats and sunglasses.

## The popup is gone

One change is not a headline feature but will be noticed every day. "No more disengagement popup in FSD v15," [Merritt posted](https://x.com/SawyerMerritt/status/2124321661075992654). Not a Tesla App confirms Tesla has completely removed the menu that asked drivers why they took over, so no prompt appears when you disengage.

Owners also get a map mode that hides the FSD visualisation, and a Calm Mode, carried over from Tesla's Robotaxis, that shows only speed, speed limit, next turn and arrival time.

## The fine print

This is still FSD (Supervised). The driver must pay attention and be ready to take over at all times. Elon Musk had said v15 would get a wide release in December, so expect more cars and models to join over the coming weeks.
