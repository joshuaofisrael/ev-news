Tesla Latinoamérica said the company will arrive in Argentina on 20 November 2026.

On [X](https://x.com/Tesla_LatAm/status/2118674774440435905), the verified Tesla Latinoamérica account posted a short video of Buenos Aires scenes with the line "Llegamos a Argentina el 20 de Noviembre." That sentence is the full official date commitment in the post. Tesla did not name models, prices, or a store format in that update.

## What local outlets report about Unicenter

[Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/economia/2026/11/06/tesla-confirmo-su-llegada-a-la-argentina-para-los-proximos-dias-los-detalles-del-desembarco-de-los-autos-electricos-de-elon-musk/) covered the same X announcement and reported that Tesla's first physical presence would be an exhibition space on Level 1 of Unicenter in Martínez, north of Buenos Aires, showing two electric vehicles. Infobae says models for that first display and Argentine retail prices have not been officially confirmed. It also reports a later stage in 2027 for a sales point and a test-drive area in parking H with up to ten cars.

[LA NACION](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/autos/tesla-confirmo-la-fecha-en-la-que-llegara-a-la-argentina-nid06112026/) likewise treated 20 November as the arrival date Tesla confirmed on X and described the Unicenter Martínez plan in similar terms. Those exhibition and 2027 sales details come from local reporting around the shopping-centre project, not from additional wording in Tesla's X post.

## What is still unconfirmed

Tesla's Latinoamérica post does not list which models will be shown or sold in Argentina, and it does not publish Argentine prices. Infobae notes that Model 3, Model Y and Cybertruck are among the possibilities discussed locally, and it cites US list-price ranges and unofficial Argentine estimates, but those are not official Argentina pricing from Tesla. Until Tesla or a regulator publishes a product and price sheet for the market, treat the news as the 20 November arrival date on X plus local-outlet reporting on the Unicenter exhibition timeline.
