Porsche delivered 38,730 battery-electric cars worldwide in the first nine months of 2026, if you add the three electric lines in its own delivery release. The [Porsche Newsroom report dated 9 October 2026](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/2026/company/porsche-deliveries-third-quarter-2026-43480.html) does not print that sum. It does print the parts: 23,029 fully electric Macans, 8,699 Taycans, and 7,002 all-electric Cayennes. Those three figures add up to 38,730. Set against the 178,532 cars Porsche says it delivered worldwide in the same period, that is 21.7 percent when the two numbers are divided and rounded to one decimal place.

The release's headline number is the all-powertrain total. Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG delivered 178,532 cars, compared with 212,509 in the first nine months of 2025. Porsche says deliveries were down 16 percent, the same rate it reported at the half-year mark, and that third-quarter deliveries followed the pattern of the first half. The company says it is sticking to a value-oriented sales approach and a balance between supply and demand.

## Where the electric cars sit inside the model lines

The Cayenne was the largest model line, at 59,586 deliveries, 2 percent below the prior year. Porsche says the all-electric Cayenne, with first customer deliveries in the summer, contributed 7,002 of those cars. The Macan line totaled 51,025, down 21 percent. Inside that line, 23,029 were the fully electric version and 27,996 were combustion-engine cars. Porsche says production of the combustion Macan ended in summer 2026.

The Taycan, the third electric line, totaled 8,699 deliveries, down 31 percent from the prior-year period. No other model in the release is described as battery-electric. The 911 rose 12 percent to 42,217. The 718 Boxster and Cayman fell 79 percent to 3,291, and Porsche says production of that line ended in October of last year. The Panamera fell 35 percent to 13,714.

Those six lines, using the figures above, add up to the 178,532 worldwide deliveries. The 38,730 figure is therefore the sum of every battery-electric line the release itemises, not a partial sample. It is still this desk's addition of Porsche's published counts. Anyone checking the claim should add 23,029, 8,699, and 7,002 on the newsroom page.

## Regions, and what Porsche says about the drop

North America was again the largest region, at 56,088 cars, down 13 percent. Porsche says that decline is mainly the end of 718 production and a strong prior-year result for the Macan Electric. Europe excluding Germany took 44,949 cars, down 11 percent. Germany took 20,954, down 7 percent. Overseas and emerging markets took 35,048, down 19 percent, which Porsche ties in part to gaps in availability of the 718 and the combustion Macan. China took 21,493, down 33 percent. Porsche says conditions there remain difficult and that it is choosing long-term brand development over short-term volume.

The regional rows in the release also add up to 178,532. The page does not split any region into electric and combustion deliveries, so the 38,730 cannot be placed by country from this document.

Matthias Becker, the Porsche AG executive board member for sales and marketing, is quoted on the mix of high-end derivatives rather than on the electric total. "This high share of our top derivatives, particularly within the 911 range, highlights the exceptional appeal of our brand," he says. He adds that customers are choosing the exclusive versions, and that this supports Porsche's place in what he calls the sporty premium and sporty luxury segments. Later in the release he points to the company's "Sportwagenschmiede '35" strategy from its capital markets day and says Porsche remains committed to value over volume.

The release also notes that GTS, Turbo, and GT models again made up a significant share of sales, and that 911 demand was strong. Those points sit beside the electric counts. They are not a claim that electric demand and 911 demand are the same phenomenon. The document's electric facts are the three model counts, the summer start of Cayenne Electric deliveries, the end of combustion Macan production, and the note that last year's Macan Electric performance is one reason North American deliveries are lower.

What the 9 October release does not do is publish a battery-electric total, a battery-electric share, or a regional electric table. The 38,730 and the 21.7 percent in this article are arithmetic from the figures Porsche did publish. The surrounding business result, which Porsche states directly, is a 16 percent decline in worldwide deliveries, to 178,532 cars, with China down 33 percent and the Cayenne still the largest line.
