BYD has staged a new twist on the old SUV-tows-a-jumbo-jet stunt. "A 180-tonne (396,000-lb) aircraft. A wet runway. Two wheels," the company [posted on X](https://x.com/BYDCompany/status/2123162748523794938) on Wednesday. "We removed one wheel from the DENZA BAO 5 (B5), and then a second. Set to pure electric mode, it pulled the full load on three wheels, then just two."

## What Denza did

According to [Denza's UK announcement](https://www.denzaukmedia.com/en/news-articles/new-premium-car-brand-in-uk,-denza,-tows-boeing-747-%E2%80%98jumbo%E2%80%99-using-its-suv-on-only-two-wheels.html), the runs took place at Dunsfold Aerodrome in Surrey. The car towed a Boeing 747-400 that left active service in 2020 but still has its engines and furnished cabin, weighing about 180 tonnes. There were three runs: on four wheels, on three, and finally on two, covering 1,600 metres in total.

Denza says the car was a standard production BAO 5 in Ultimate trim with minimal changes. Engineers fitted a bespoke towing attachment, took out passenger seats to add 2,000kg of ballast for the three-wheel run and 4,500kg for the two-wheel run, and added small stabilisers to each axle. The DMO plug-in hybrid powertrain was left unchanged.

Denza calls it the first time a production car has towed a plane on two wheels, and the first to tow a 747 on electric power alone. Those are the brand's own claims. Torrential rain on the day delayed the final attempt until the runway dried enough for traction, and Denza says post-run checks found no stress on the powertrain, chassis or tyres.

## The car behind it

The BAO 5, sold in some markets as the Denza B5, uses three electric motors (one on each axle plus a generator unit) and a 1.5-litre turbocharged petrol engine. Denza UK quotes 544PS, 760Nm, 56 miles of electric range and 537 miles combined. We cover more of the group on our [BYD tag page](/tags/byd/).

## Keep it in proportion

As [Auto Trader NZ points out](https://autotrader.co.nz/news/watch-denza-b5-towa-a-jumbo-jet-with-two-wheels-removed), Volkswagen did a similar 747 tow with a Touareg in 2006, the plane's weight sat on its own landing gear, and the B5's real towing ratings for caravans and boats are 3,000kg braked and 750kg unbraked. This was a low-speed pull under controlled conditions, not a towing rating.

## Our read

Analysis: the two-wheel run is a clever way to show off the B5's separate front and rear motors, which can send all their torque to whatever wheels remain. It says more about traction control and torque than about everyday towing.
