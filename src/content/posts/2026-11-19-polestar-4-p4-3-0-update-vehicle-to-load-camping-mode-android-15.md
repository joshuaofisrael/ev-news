Polestar is sending its P4.3.0 over-the-air update to every Polestar 4, adding Vehicle-to-Load (V2L) power, a Camping mode, a Valet mode and Android Automotive OS 15, [The EV Report says](https://theevreport.com/polestar-4-ota-update-android-15-vehicle-to-load), citing Polestar's 18 November announcement. Polestar UK managing director Matt Galvin called it the company's biggest over-the-air software release.

## Power out of the battery

With a compatible adapter in the charging port, the Polestar 4 can now supply up to 3.3 kW from its battery to camping kit, tools or electronics. Polestar says V2L will reach most of its markets, but it will not be offered in the US, Canada or South Korea.

Camping mode keeps the cabin temperature and infotainment running and leaves the main power outlets active, for owners sleeping in the car or charging phones and laptops overnight.

## The rest of the update

- **Digital key on Android:** compatible Google and Samsung phones can now lock, unlock and start the car. iPhone support arrived in 2025, and activation is rolling out market by market.
- **Park Assist Pilot:** on 2026 and 2027 model-year cars with the Pilot pack, the car can steer and brake into selected perpendicular spaces once the driver confirms.
- **Valet mode:** restricted access, adjustable speed limits and a locked glovebox.
- **Android Automotive OS 15:** faster infotainment and start-up, better remote controls and digital key performance, improved Google Maps energy estimates, and updates to active safety and driver assistance systems.

There are also more home-screen shortcuts, revised climate controls and easier switching between the Dashcam and Gallery apps. New Polestar 4s are delivered with P4.3.0 already installed.

"Polestar 4 is our best-selling model with over sixteen and half thousand on the road today," Galvin said in the announcement.
