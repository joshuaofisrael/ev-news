Nissan's Formula E team has started the GEN4 era of the all-electric championship at official pre-season testing at Circuito del Jarama in Madrid, the company [said on 16 November](https://global.nissannews.com/en/releases/nissan-formula-e-team-to-launch-gen4-era-at-madrid-pre-season-testing). Oliver Rowland and Zane Maloney are the team's driver pairing for the new car.

## The test week

Teams have three days of running scheduled this week, on Monday 16, Wednesday 18 and Friday 20 November, with gaps between them to fine-tune their cars, according to Nissan. The team is running a one-off testing livery. The full 2027 season livery will be revealed at the NISMO Festival at Fuji Speedway in Japan on 6 December.

## A bigger calendar

Nissan says the 2027 season has a record 21 races, the most in Formula E's 13-year history, with three new venues: Circuit of the Americas, Brands Hatch and Circuit Zandvoort. GEN4 runs from the 2027 season to 2030, and Nissan says it was the first manufacturer to sign up for it, in March 2024.

## The drivers

Maloney, from Barbados, makes his first official outing for the team and will race as number 22. Rowland, who won the drivers' title in season 11, returns to number 23 after a season running number 1. Reserve and development driver Sam Bird said the GEN4 car "is very different to what we've experienced before in Formula E" and that the team expects much quicker lap times in Madrid than it set with GEN3.

## Why it matters for road cars

Nissan says knowledge from racing feeds into its road EVs, and that by the early 2030s all new Nissan models in key markets will be electrified. Testing times in Madrid will give the first real sense of how the new cars compare, though teams often run different programmes in pre-season, so the times are only a rough guide.
