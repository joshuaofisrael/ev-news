[Tesla Manufacturing's account on X](https://x.com/gigafactories/status/2113745614228099452) claims Giga Berlin built 7,000 Model Y in one week. The post this desk opened reads: "Ramp-up is in full swing at Giga Berlin. This week we hit the next production milestone: 7,000 Model Y built in one week. Congrats to the whole team!" The page dates that post 23 October 2026.

[TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/23/tesla-giga-berlin-7000-model-y-week/) reports the same claim. It prints that sentence and attributes it to the Tesla Manufacturing account, in a post TeslaNorth dates 23 October 2026. TeslaNorth is reporting the account's post. It is not a Tesla production filing.

The 7,000 figure is unverified. Tesla's [Q3 2026 Update](https://assets-ir.tesla.com/tesla-contents/IR/TSLA-Q3-2026-Update.pdf) says the company "attained record production at Gigafactory Berlin" and lists Berlin Model Y capacity as more than 375,000 a year. It does not state that the plant built 7,000 Model Y in one week. The update schedules its results webcast for 21 October 2026, before the post.
