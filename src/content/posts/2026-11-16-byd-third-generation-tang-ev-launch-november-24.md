BYD will officially launch the third-generation Tang on 24 November, [CnEVPost reports](https://cnevpost.com/2026/11/16/byd-to-launch-3rd-gen-tang-nov-24/), citing the Chinese car site Yiche. The launch comes 11 days after pre-sales began.

## Prices and versions

Pre-sales opened on 13 November with three versions: two single-motor rear-wheel-drive models at 225,000 yuan ($33,510) and 240,000 yuan, and a dual-motor all-wheel-drive model at 260,000 yuan, according to CnEVPost.

## Range and charging

The rear-drive versions use a 300 kW (402 hp) motor and are rated at up to 850 km (528 miles) on China's CLTC cycle. The all-wheel-drive version has 515 kW of combined power, does 0 to 100 km/h in 4.3 seconds and is rated at 770 km. CLTC figures are typically higher than European WLTP or US EPA ratings.

The SUV uses BYD's second-generation Blade Battery and flash charging, which BYD says can take the battery from 10% to 70% in about five minutes and to 97% in about nine minutes at room temperature.

## Size and cabin

The new Tang is 5,045 mm long and 1,980 mm wide on a 2,950 mm wheelbase. CnEVPost says the cabin combines a 35.4-inch display with a 30-inch panoramic screen, and all four main seats have ventilation, heating and massage. BYD's lidar-equipped God's Eye B driver-assistance system and dual-chamber air suspension are standard, though final specifications will come with the launch configuration sheet.

## Why it matters for BYD

The five-seat Tang sits alongside the seven-seat Da Tang launched in June as a pair of flagship SUVs in BYD's Dynasty range. Tang family sales rose 15.24% year on year to 16,664 in October, but were down 25.28% to 98,278 over January to October, according to CnEVPost's data.
