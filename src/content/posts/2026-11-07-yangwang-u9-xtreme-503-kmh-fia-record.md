BYD's electric Yangwang U9 Xtreme has reached 503.17 km/h (312.65 mph), making it the first production car to go faster than 500 km/h. The FIA, motorsport's world governing body, has recognised the speed.

BYD Europe [announced the run on X](https://x.com/BYD_Europe/status/2118607070450655711):

> "A new world record for the YANGWANG U9 Xtreme. 503.17km/h. YANGWANG U9 Xtreme has done the impossible, surpassing 500km/h to set a new world speed record in Abu Dhabi."

## How the run was done

The run took place on 1 November 2026 on a closed section of the Sheikh Khalifa Bin Zayed International Road (E11) in Abu Dhabi's Al Dhafra region, [according to the FIA](https://www.fia.com/news/yangwang-u9-xtreme-surpasses-500-kmh-speed-achievement-recognised-fia). German racing driver Marc Basseng was at the wheel, as he was for the car's earlier 496.22 km/h run at the ATP Papenburg proving ground in Germany in September 2025.

Local authorities closed a 10 km stretch of road, including a seven-kilometre straight. Two runs on 31 October peaked at 490 km/h. Engineers then changed the aerodynamics, suspension settings and tyre pressures, and the second of two runs on 1 November reached the final figure, the FIA said.

## What the records cover

[Guinness World Records](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/791227-highest-speed-achieved-by-an-electric-car-in-a-single-direction-run) awarded two titles: the highest speed achieved by a production car and by an electric production car in a single-direction run. Guinness says the speed was checked using the car's speedometer and a calibrated VBOX data logger, with an FIA representative reviewing the data.

The records are for a peak speed in one direction. Guinness notes they are not the same as conventional fastest-production-car records, which average runs made in opposite directions to cancel out wind and gradient.

The U9 Xtreme is a limited-run version of Yangwang's electric supercar. It uses four electric motors with a combined output of more than 3,000 PS and a 1,200-volt electrical system, and production is capped at 30 cars, [Grande Prêmio reported](https://grandepremio.com/en/cars/fia-recognises-byd-yangwang-u9-xtremes-503-17-km-h-production-car-speed-record/).
