The Cybercab has left Austin. "Cybercab expands to second city," [Tesla posted on X](https://x.com/Tesla/status/2123507319040528546) on Thursday. Earlier that day Sawyer Merritt [reported](https://x.com/SawyerMerritt/status/2123450073170268400) that Tesla had announced "Cybercab rides are officially launching for the public in Dallas, Texas, making this Tesla's first Cybercab ride expansion to a new city beyond Austin."

## What riders get

According to [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/news/816139/tesla-cybercab-robotaxi-expands-to-dallas/), Dallas is the second major city for the purpose-built Cybercab after its Austin launch in September. The two-door car carries up to two passengers and has no steering wheel or pedals. Riders book it through the Tesla Robotaxi app.

Tesla already ran Model Y robotaxis in Dallas, so this adds a new vehicle to an existing service rather than opening a new market. Merritt also noted that the Dallas Cybercabs "have Starlink installed directly on the back of the vehicle from the factory."

## Where the rest of the fleet stands

InsideEVs lists Tesla robotaxi service in Houston, Fort Worth, Tampa, San Antonio, Orlando and Miami as well as Austin and Dallas, all using the Model Y apart from the two Cybercab cities. The San Francisco Bay Area service still has safety drivers because of California rules. Our [Fort Worth launch story](/articles/2026-11-03-tesla-robotaxi-fort-worth-launch/) covers one of those Model Y expansions.

## Why it matters

The Cybercab is the vehicle Tesla's robotaxi plans depend on. Elon Musk has talked about building millions, and Tesla plans to wind down Cybertruck production in Austin next summer to free up room for it, as we reported in our [Cybertruck wind-down story](/articles/2026-11-06-musk-cybertruck-wind-down-cybercab/). Two cities is a small start, and InsideEVs urges caution on Tesla's big numbers given its record on past targets.

Tesla has also offered the Cybercab to private buyers, tied to a $299-a-month autonomy subscription, as covered in our [Cybercab orders story](/articles/2026-11-15-tesla-cybercab-personal-orders-29990-299-subscription/). How a car without manual controls meets federal safety standards is still unclear.

Tesla has not said how many Cybercabs are running in Dallas or how large the service area is.
