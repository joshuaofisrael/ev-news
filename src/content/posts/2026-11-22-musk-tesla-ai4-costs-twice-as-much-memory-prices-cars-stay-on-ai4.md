The chip shortage hitting the AI industry has reached Tesla's cars. "AI4 costs twice as much now due to memory and storage price hikes," Elon Musk wrote on X on Sunday, [according to TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/22/tesla-ai4-computer-costs-double/).

He also answered the question many owners have been asking about the next chip. "It's going to Optimus. Cars will remain at AI4. That's enough to solve superhuman driving," [Musk posted](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124475458150334900), replying to a driver who asked when AI5 would reach new cars.

## What AI4 is

AI4, also called Hardware 4, is the computer behind Tesla's driver-assistance and self-driving software. It is the hardware getting the first wave of FSD (Supervised) v15.0.1, which began rolling out this weekend.

TeslaNorth says Musk made the cost comment in a thread about how much computing power Tesla's new Reality View visualisation uses. He did not give a dollar figure or say when the increase started. TeslaNorth notes the comment was about Tesla's hardware cost, not a change to what buyers pay for a car, an FSD subscription or a retrofit.

## Why memory matters

Memory and storage chips are in short supply as AI data centres buy them in huge volumes, and prices have risen sharply this year. A self-driving computer needs a lot of fast memory to run large neural networks in real time, so it is exposed to the same price swings.

For Tesla, a more expensive computer in every car squeezes margins unless it raises prices or finds savings elsewhere. Musk has not said what Tesla will do.

## AI5 skips cars, for now

TeslaNorth reports that in April Musk said Tesla was working on an AI4 upgrade with newer memory while prioritising AI5 for Optimus robots and data centres. Sunday's reply confirms that plan for now, but Musk did not say how long cars will stay on AI4.

His claim that AI4 is "enough to solve superhuman driving" is a goal, not a result. FSD remains a supervised system, and drivers must stay attentive and ready to take over.

## What it means for buyers

If you are waiting for an "AI5 Tesla" before buying, Musk's answer is that there is no such car planned for now. Cars sold today ship with AI4, the same hardware getting v15 first.
