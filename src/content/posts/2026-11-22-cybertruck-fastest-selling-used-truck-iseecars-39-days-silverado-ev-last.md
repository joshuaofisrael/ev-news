For a truck that drew so many jokes at launch, the Cybertruck is moving fast on the used market. An [X post on Saturday](https://x.com/XFreeze/status/2124041754278277534) highlighted new data showing it is now the fastest-selling used pickup in America.

## The numbers

The [iSeeCars study](https://www.iseecars.com/articles/teslas-cybertruck-is-the-fastest-selling-used-truck) analysed more than 1.1 million sales of one- to five-year-old used vehicles in October 2026. The Cybertruck averaged 39.1 days on the market, against 65.5 days for used trucks overall. That is 26.4 days faster than the average pickup.

The top five used trucks by days on market were:

- Tesla Cybertruck: 39.1 days
- Ford Maverick hybrid: 43.3 days
- Rivian R1T: 45.2 days
- Ford F-150 Lightning: 47.4 days
- Toyota Tacoma: 50.1 days

Electric pickups took three of the top four places and four of the top 10, with the GMC Sierra EV ninth.

## Not every electric truck is flying off lots

The Chevrolet Silverado EV came last of the 24 trucks in the study, at 107.5 days. That is 68.4 days longer than the Cybertruck and 42 days longer than the average truck. Being electric alone does not make a used truck sell quickly.

## EVs in general are selling faster

iSeeCars found used EVs averaged 45.6 days on the market, against 54.5 days for hybrids and 61.0 days for gasoline vehicles. The overall average was 59.5 days. EVs took 16 of the 25 fastest-selling spots across all vehicle types.

Three Teslas made that overall top 25: the Cybertruck in 16th, the Model 3 in 18th at 39.5 days and the Model Y in 24th at 39.8 days. The fastest-selling used vehicle overall was the Audi A4 allroad at 33.7 days.

## What it means if you are buying or selling

Days on market measures how quickly cars sell, not what they sell for. A fast sale can mean strong demand, or prices that have already fallen enough to attract buyers. If you are selling a used Cybertruck, the data suggests you will not wait long. If you are shopping for one, expect competition for good examples.
