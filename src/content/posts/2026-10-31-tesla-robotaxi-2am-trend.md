What people are discussing on X this weekend is Tesla's driverless ride service staying open later. This desk opened a trending page titled ["Tesla Robotaxi Extends Hours to 2 a.m. with New Rider Features"](https://x.com/i/trending/2115598446799274122), which showed a last-updated date of 30 October 2026. X describes these pages as summaries of posts that may change. The page shows the topic is trending. It is not the source for the facts below, which come from Tesla's own announcement as reported by three Tesla news sites.

According to [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/29/robotaxi-hours-2am-calm-mode-share/), Tesla's official @robotaxi account posted a service update late on Thursday, 29 October, saying operating hours are now 6 a.m. to 2 a.m. That is a 20-hour daily window. TeslaNorth notes that the post does not name which cities the new hours cover. Tesla runs its Robotaxi service in Texas, and TeslaNorth reports recent rider offers in Dallas and Houston.

The same update listed several rider features, as reported by TeslaNorth and [TrendingTesla](https://trendingtesla.com/tesla-robotaxi-hours-2am-calm-mode-trip-sharing-grok-model-y/):

- **Rear camera view:** riders can watch the rear camera feed during the trip.
- **Trip sharing:** riders can share their trip details with a friend.
- **Live Activity:** a redesigned status view on the phone's lock screen.
- **Calm Mode:** dims the screen and sets the cabin lighting to a relaxing state.
- **Grok:** Tesla's AI assistant is now available in Robotaxi Model Y vehicles.

TeslaNorth says the update also included accessibility features.

## How the hours have grown

The cut-off has moved quickly. [Drive Tesla Canada](https://driveteslacanada.ca/news/tesla-robotaxi-2am-operating-hours-new-features/) reports that Tesla pushed its closing time from 11 p.m. to midnight on 17 October, then to 1 a.m. the following week, and now to 2 a.m., which it describes as going from 11 p.m. to 2 a.m. in 12 days. TrendingTesla reports that in late August, Austin rides ran from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m.

TeslaNorth reports that on Tesla's third-quarter earnings call, Ashok Elluswamy, who leads AI software at Tesla, said a 2 a.m. window was coming soon. After the update, Elluswamy posted on X: "Until bar closing time now!"

## What to watch

**Analysis:** Later hours matter for a ride service because late-night trips, after shifts, events and nights out, are a real share of demand. The open questions are which cities the 2 a.m. hours apply to, and whether the service will move to round-the-clock operation, which TeslaNorth reports Elluswamy pointed toward on the earnings call. Tesla has not given a date for that. Some posts in the X discussion cite fleet and safety numbers; this desk has not verified those figures and does not repeat them.
