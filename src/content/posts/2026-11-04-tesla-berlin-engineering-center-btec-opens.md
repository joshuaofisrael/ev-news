Tesla has opened its Berlin Tesla Engineering Center (BTEC) in the Köpenick district of Berlin, bringing four engineering sites together on a single campus about 30 minutes from its Giga Berlin factory in Grünheide.

Tesla Europe [announced the opening on X](https://x.com/teslaeurope/status/2118066010381308279) on 4 November:

> "Berlin Tesla Engineering Center opens today. From permit to operational in under a year, it brings 4 engineering sites onto 1 campus w/ 30 different labs in Köpenick, 30 minutes from Giga Berlin. With our 3rd hub of this kind after Fremont & Shanghai, we're combining materials, powertrain, vehicle engineering & validation under one roof – enabling a short feedback loop between development & ramp"

That makes Berlin Tesla's third engineering hub of this kind, after Fremont in California and Shanghai in China.

## What happens at the campus

The centre combines materials, powertrain, vehicle engineering and validation work under one roof, according to Tesla. Its 30 labs include facilities for airbag safety tests and battery-cell tests, [rbb24 reported](https://www.rbb24.de/wirtschaft/beitrag/2026/11/tesla-eroeffnet-entwicklungszentrum-berlin-koepenick-eauto.html), citing information from Tesla via the dpa news agency.

Tesla's argument is that putting development close to the factory shortens the feedback loop between engineering changes and the production ramp at Giga Berlin.

## Staff and investment

More than 250 people will work at the centre in its first phase, according to the company's figures reported by dpa. Many have moved from Tesla's site in Tilburg in the Netherlands and from other lab locations.

Tesla has already filed a permit application for a second phase. If that goes ahead, the campus could eventually employ up to around 500 engineers and technicians. The total investment is put at about €100 million.

## A former cable factory

BTEC occupies converted halls of a former cable factory on Friedrichshagener Straße. The [Tagesspiegel reported](https://www.tagesspiegel.de/berlin/us-autobauer-tesla-eroffnet-entwicklungszentrum-in-berlin-16242531.html) that parts of the site had already been operating since late September, so this week's event was the formal opening rather than the first day of work. Berlin's mayor, Kai Wegner, attended the opening.

Tesla said the project went from permit to operation in under a year.

## Part of a busy week for Tesla in Germany

The opening comes on the same day as new registration figures showed [Tesla's German registrations nearly quadrupled in October](/articles/2026-11-04-tesla-germany-registrations-nearly-quadruple-october-2026/) compared with a year earlier, though from a low base and well down on September.

The next milestone for BTEC is the second-phase permit, which would decide whether the campus grows towards its planned size of around 500 engineering staff.
