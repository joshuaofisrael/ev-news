Tesla's new car registrations in Germany nearly quadrupled in October 2026 from a year earlier, according to figures from Germany's Federal Motor Transport Authority (KBA) [reported by Reuters via Euronext](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/teslas-new-registrations-germany-nearly-quadruple-october-kba-says). Tesla registered 2,932 cars in the month, up 290.9%.

From January to October, Tesla's German registrations rose 206.1% to 47,742.

## Faster than the wider EV market

Germany's battery-electric registrations also grew strongly, rising 79.5% to 94,086 in October, Reuters reported. Even so, Tesla's share of all new registrations in the month was 1.2%, behind BYD at 2.0% and Leapmotor at 1.4%, with Xpeng at 0.5%.

The overall German car market grew far more slowly. KBA press release 44/2026, published on 4 November, put total new car registrations at 254,492, up 1.7%, according to [Mein Tesla Blog](https://mein-tesla-blog.de/blog/aktuelles/tesla-neuzulassungen-oktober-2026/). Registrations by private owners rose 20.6% and made up 40.7% of the total.

## A quieter month than September

The year-on-year jump hides a big drop from the month before. Mein Tesla Blog's figures show Tesla registered 12,552 cars in Germany in September, so October's 2,932 was about 77% lower month on month.

## Berlin production claims

Tesla commentator Sawyer Merritt [posted on X](https://x.com/SawyerMerritt/status/2117972065881850311): "Tesla's car registrations in Germany were up 290% YoY in Germany in October." In the same post he said Gigafactory Berlin built 7,000 Model Ys in one week a couple of weeks ago, that the target is 7,500 a week, and that more than 2,000 new jobs are planned. Those production and jobs claims come from his post and have not been independently verified.

Tesla's supervised self-driving software is not yet approved in Germany, according to Mein Tesla Blog, which noted that the Dutch regulator RDW approved changes on 3 November.

Whether October's year-on-year growth turns into steadily higher monthly volumes will only be clear once more months of KBA data are in.
